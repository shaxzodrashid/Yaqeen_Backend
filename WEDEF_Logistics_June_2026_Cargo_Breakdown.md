## 4\. Key Operational, Pricing & Accounting Insights

### 1\. Currency Conversion & RMB Mechanics

- **Fixed Internal Exchange Benchmarks:** For China-based freight forwarding, RMB rates were strictly fixed at **6.75 RMB/USD** for standard sea/rail shipments from Taizhou, Foshan, Chaozhou, Hebei, and Jinhua. Specialized routes utilized **6.72 RMB/USD** (Beijing rail/road) and **6.80 RMB/USD** (Zhongshan consolidated line-haul).

### 2\. Profit Margin Deductions & Commission Structure

- **Partner Commissions & Bonuses:** Gross margins (`Transport Price − Transport Cost`) frequently reflect deductions for field partner commissions and performance bonuses. Most notably, **Fazliddin aka** received sales/partner commissions across multiple containers (e.g., \#1: $50 commission \+ $50 bonus \+ $150 expenses; \#2 & \#7: $100 commission \+ $50 bonus; \#15, \#63, \#70, \#72: $100 commission \+ $50 bonus; \#16: $550 commission \+ $550 bonus; \#59: $150 commission \+ $50 bonus).

- **Transit Surcharges:** A standard 0.4% insurance and transit risk surcharge was factored on declared commercial invoice values (e.g., \#29 Muzaffar aka chinni; \#44 Shamsiddin aka: $300 surcharge; \#56 Husan aka truba: $318 surcharge on $79,312 invoice).
- **Local Clearance & Delivery Surcharges:** LTL groupage consignments (\#50 and \#51 for AIGUL) incurred direct terminal-to-warehouse delivery charges ($13 and 400,000 UZS respectively), which were factored into the net margins.
